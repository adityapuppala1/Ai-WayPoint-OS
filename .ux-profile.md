# Waypoint UX profile

Read this before changing layout, colour or type. It records the choices behind the
Waypoint design system and the patterns this product never uses. Full rationale lives in
`docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`; tokens live in `packages/tokens`.

## Concept: wayfinding signage + route lines

People open Waypoint in transit: between jobs, countries, diagnoses, life stages. Often
stressed, often reading in a second language, often on a cheap phone in bright sunlight.
Airport and transit wayfinding solves exactly that problem, so the interface borrows its
discipline:

- **Signs** answer "where do I go now?" One sign per screen, at most. The Next Step sign on
  Today is the product's one bold, memorable element.
- **Route lines** show sequences (plans, onboarding, progress). Stations are steps. They are
  information, never decoration.
- **Line colours** identify modules the way transit lines do. They appear only on small
  marks (station dots, pictogram tint, active-nav bar, chart series), never as large fills.
- **Pictograms + words**, always together. Icons never carry meaning alone.

## Surfaces (60 · 30 · 10)

| Role | Light | Dark | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L0 canvas | `--wp-canvas` (cool concrete) | slate night | page background, ~60% |
| L1 raised | white, 1px border (slate 11%), elevation 1 | a clearly lighter slate (1.17:1 from the canvas), lit top edge | lists, panels, nav, ~30% |
| L2 overlay | white, 1px border, elevation 3 | lighter again (1.24:1 from raised), faint ring | menus, dialogs, sheets |
| Sign | slate panel, white text, elevation 2 | lit: a brighter slate at 3:1 against the canvas, wider signal edge | Next Step sign only |
| Accent | signal yellow `--wp-signal` | same | primary CTA fill, focus halo, ~10% max |

- **Elevation** has three steps and no more: 1 rests on the page (`--wp-shadow-1`, two soft
  layers, never above 8% black), 2 is lifted (the Sign, a card under the pointer), 3 floats
  (`--wp-shadow-3`, up to 14%). In the dark a shadow hardly shows, so depth comes from the
  surface colour and a lit edge.
- The Sign must be the boldest shape on the page in both themes: 3:1 or more against the
  canvas. The token test enforces it.
- Links are slate text with an underline; hover/focus gives a yellow highlight (never yellow text).
- Focus: 3px signal-yellow halo plus 2px ring in the text colour, from one token
  (`--wp-focus-shadow`). Two colours, so one of them is visible on every surface. Flush
  controls (rows, tabs) draw the same ring inside their edge.
- The marker under the chosen tab or nav item is `--wp-marker` (slate in the light, near
  white in the dark). Signal yellow may sit beside it but never marks a choice alone: on a
  light page it is 1.4:1.
- Lists and tables always sit in an L1 panel with hairline row dividers.

## States

- Everything that can be pressed answers the press at once: it gives a little (scale 0.97)
  or takes a shade (`--wp-fill-pressed`). On touch this is the only feedback there is.
- Hover styles apply only where a pointer hovers (`@media (hover: hover)`), so nothing stays
  lit after a tap.
- State changes take 120ms.

## Type

- One family: **Overpass** (open-source, derived from Highway Gothic road signage).
  Script fallbacks: Noto Sans Devanagari / Bengali / Arabic (unicode-range, so they only
  load when needed), then system fonts.
- Weights: 400 body, 500 labels & buttons, 600 headings & signs. Nothing above 600.
- Sizes (rem at 16px root): meta 0.75 · secondary 0.875 · body 1 · lead 1.1875 ·
  h4 1.4375 · h3 1.75 · h2 2.0625 · h1 2.5 · sign 3.  Minor-third steps.
- 12px is meta only (timestamps, legal, helper). List rows and form values are 14–16px.
- Line-height: headings 1.15–1.25; body 1.55; meta 1.4.
- Numbers use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`), not a monospace face.

## Never

- Cream background + serif display + terracotta accent.
- Near-black canvas with a single acid accent.
- ALL-CAPS eyebrow labels above headings; letter-spaced small caps labels.
- Metadata joined with middle dots ("A · B · C") in the UI — use separate lines or a
  definition list. (Docs may use dots; product UI may not.)
- "WORD — fragment" labels; "→" appended to link or button text.
- Monospace for small data labels.
- Grids of identical rounded cards with the same shadow; gradient washes; glassmorphism;
  rainbow or multi-hue gradients on buttons.
- Cartoon people or mascots in empty states. Empty states = one sentence + one action.
- Decorative icons inside inputs (allowed: search, clear, show password).
- Pills around static metadata. Chips only when they are interactive filters or status.
- Streaks, loss-aversion copy, infinite feeds, confetti. Progress is shown as a route.
- Alarmist red outside true danger (scam = danger; a crisis moment = calm harbour blue).
- More than one Sign per screen.
- Motion that plays by itself to draw the eye, loops, or asks for attention. Motion answers
  something the person did, or shows a thing arriving once.

## Motion

- 120–240ms, ease-out for entrances, ease-in for exits.
- The one orchestrated moment: when a step is completed, the route fills to the next
  station (480ms, `--wp-duration-route`), the dot settles there, and the Next Step sign
  flips to the new step (split-flap). This is the only movement meant to be watched.
- Everything else is a direct response to a user action and keeps things where the eye
  left them: the marker slides to the chosen tab, a sheet leaves the way it came, a
  disclosure opens by height, a toast rises in and offers Undo, a meter grows to its value
  when it appears.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: all transitions become instant cross-fades ≤80ms or none.

## Global legibility

- Logical CSS properties everywhere (`margin-inline-start`, not `margin-left`) — RTL works.
- Minimum touch target 44×44px. Minimum contrast 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for UI graphics.
- Lite mode: system fonts, no images, no motion, fewer requests.

## Changes to this profile

Rules changed on purpose, and why. Every "Never" item stands.

- **Shadows (was: "soft shadow (<0.08)", overlays only).** Now a three-step elevation
  scale, panels included. Surfaces were 1.10:1 apart and the interface read as flat; a
  two-layer shadow at 6% and 4% gives panels an edge without looking heavy. The ceiling for
  floating surfaces is 14%.
- **The Sign in the dark (was: "same").** It was 1.36:1 against the dark canvas, one more
  panel among panels. It is now a brighter slate at 3:1 with a wider signal edge.
- **Dark surfaces (was: "+1 step", "+2 steps" at 1.08:1 each).** The steps are now about
  1.2:1, so a panel can be told from the page without relying on a hairline.
- **Active marker (was: signal yellow).** Yellow alone is 1.4:1 on a light page. The marker
  is now one colour with 3:1 everywhere.
- **Motion (was: one moment, everything else unspecified).** The route fill that this
  profile always promised now exists and has its own duration (480ms), outside the
  120–240ms range like the 320ms flip it accompanies. Continuity motion is named, and
  attention-seeking motion is added to "Never".
